# Southeast Asia Travel Guide: Tours in Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia and Cambodia

An open guide to planning a trip through mainland and island Southeast Asia, maintained by the team behind [JetlyGo, the free trip planner](https://jetlygo.com/). It covers where to go, when to go, and which tours are actually worth booking in four countries that most travellers combine on one trip.

Everything here is written in plain English and kept short enough to read on a phone at the airport. There is no code in this repository. It is a living set of travel notes, and it gets updated whenever our tour pages, seasons or entry rules change.

## Quick picks by country

**Vietnam.** Start with an overnight boat in the north. The [Ha Long Bay overnight cruises](https://jetlygo.com/tours/vietnam/ha-long-bay/overnight-cruises) compare cabins, routes and what each boat includes. In the centre, Hoi An is the easiest base for half-day trips, and the guide to [Vietnam tours and cruises](https://jetlygo.com/tours/vietnam) lists everything from cooking classes to multi-day caving.

**Thailand.** Chiang Mai is the north's adventure capital, with ethical elephant sanctuaries, white water and mountain biking. In the south, [Phuket island and lake tours](https://jetlygo.com/tours/thailand/phuket) cover Phi Phi, Phang Nga Bay and the floating raft houses of Cheow Lan Lake. See all options on the [Thailand tours hub](https://jetlygo.com/tours/thailand).

**Indonesia.** The country is enormous, so pick a region. Divers head east for [Komodo National Park liveaboards](https://jetlygo.com/tours/indonesia/labuan-bajo), while first-timers usually start with [Bali day trips and surf lessons](https://jetlygo.com/tours/indonesia/bali). The full list sits on the [Indonesia tours page](https://jetlygo.com/tours/indonesia).

**Cambodia.** Most people come for Angkor and stay longer than planned. Beyond the temples, the [Siem Reap countryside tours](https://jetlygo.com/tours/cambodia/siem-reap) get you out to rice fields and villages that tour buses never reach. Browse more on the [Cambodia tours page](https://jetlygo.com/tours/cambodia).

## How we choose what to recommend

1. **We work with the operator directly.** No tour appears here unless we have spoken with the people running it.
2. **Like-for-like prices.** A price is for the trip as described, for your group size, on your dates. No "from" numbers that only apply to a group of twelve in the rainy season.
3. **Plain language.** If a tour description needs a glossary, we rewrite it.
4. **Honest seasons.** Every guide says when a trip is a bad idea, not only when it is a good one.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which country should I visit first?**
For a first trip to the region, Thailand is the gentlest start: frequent flights, easy transport and tourism infrastructure everywhere. Vietnam rewards a little more planning with some of the most varied scenery in Asia. Our [multi-country itineraries](southeast-asia-itineraries.md) show how to combine them.

**What is the best time to visit Southeast Asia?**
Roughly November to April is dry for most of mainland Southeast Asia, but central Vietnam and parts of Indonesia run on different calendars. The [trip planning checklist](travel-planning-checklist.md) has a month-by-month table.

**Do I need a visa?**
It depends on your passport. You can [check visa requirements by passport](https://jetlygo.com/visa) on JetlyGo before you book anything non-refundable.

**Can I book these tours directly?**
Yes. Every tour linked in these guides can be booked on [jetlygo.com](https://jetlygo.com/), and you can ask questions before paying.
