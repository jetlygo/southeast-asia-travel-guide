# Southeast Asia Trip Planning Checklist

[Back to the Southeast Asia travel guide](README.md)

A short, practical list to work through before you travel to Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia or Cambodia. It is ordered by what needs doing first.

## 1. Check entry rules for every country

Visa rules change often and depend entirely on your passport. Some nationalities enter visa-free, some need an e-visa before departure, and some can get a visa on arrival. Before you buy flights, [check visa requirements by passport](https://jetlygo.com/visa) for each country on your route.

Things that catch travellers out:

- **Passport validity.** All four countries generally expect at least six months left on your passport from the date you arrive.
- **Onward tickets.** Airlines may refuse boarding if you cannot show a ticket out of the country.
- **Transit stops.** A layover in a third country can need its own visa. JetlyGo has a separate [transit visa checker](https://jetlygo.com/transit-visa) for connecting flights.
- **Overland crossings.** Not every land border issues e-visas. Check the specific crossing, not only the country.

## 2. Pick your months

| Month | Vietnam north | Vietnam centre | Thailand | Bali | Cambodia |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jan | Cool, dry | Showers | Excellent | Wet | Excellent |
| Feb | Misty | Good | Excellent | Wet | Excellent |
| Mar | Warm | Excellent | Hot | Showers | Hot |
| Apr | Warm | Excellent | Very hot | Good | Very hot |
| May | Hot | Excellent | Rains start | Excellent | Hot, rains start |
| Jun | Hot, wet | Excellent | Wet | Excellent | Wet |
| Jul | Hot, wet | Excellent | Wet | Excellent, busy | Wet |
| Aug | Hot, wet | Good | Wet | Excellent, busy | Wet |
| Sep | Good | Rainy | Wet | Excellent | Wet |
| Oct | Excellent | Floods possible | Rains ending | Good | Wet |
| Nov | Excellent | Floods possible | Good | Showers | Good |
| Dec | Cool | Rainy | Excellent | Wet | Excellent |

There is no single best month for all four countries, which is why most long trips route around the weather. The [multi-country itineraries](southeast-asia-itineraries.md) are built with this in mind.

## 3. Book in the right order

1. **International flights.** Compare [flights to Southeast Asia](https://jetlygo.com/flights) and fly into one city and out of another if it saves backtracking.
2. **Anything with limited spaces.** Liveaboards in [Komodo](https://jetlygo.com/tours/indonesia/labuan-bajo) and [Raja Ampat](https://jetlygo.com/tours/indonesia/raja-ampat), expedition caves in [Phong Nha](https://jetlygo.com/tours/vietnam/phong-nha), and peak-season [Ha Long Bay cruises](https://jetlygo.com/tours/vietnam/ha-long-bay).
3. **Accommodation for the first and last nights.** Leave the middle flexible if you like.
4. **Day tours.** Most can be booked a few days ahead, and weather-dependent boat trips are better booked once you are there.

## 4. Money

- **Cash still matters.** Markets, street food, tuk-tuks and small guesthouses are cash only in all four countries.
- **Cards work in cities.** Hotels, malls and larger restaurants take cards, often with a small surcharge.
- **US dollars in Cambodia.** Cambodia uses dollars alongside the riel, and prices are commonly quoted in dollars.
- **Big notes are a problem.** Break large notes at supermarkets; small vendors rarely have change.

## 5. Getting around

| Country | Best way between cities |
|---|---|
| Vietnam | Domestic flights for long hops; trains and sleeper buses for short ones |
| Thailand | Domestic flights, overnight trains, and ferries to the islands |
| Indonesia | Domestic flights between islands; fast boats from Bali to Lombok and the Gilis |
| Cambodia | Buses and minivans; flights from Siem Reap to Phnom Penh |

Ride-hailing apps such as Grab work in all four countries' main cities and avoid haggling over fares.

## 6. Health and safety

- See a travel doctor around six to eight weeks before departure about vaccinations.
- Use mosquito repellent at dawn and dusk; dengue is present across the region.
- Buy travel insurance that covers the activities you plan, especially diving, motorbiking and trekking.
- Drink bottled or filtered water.

## 7. Respect local customs

- Cover shoulders and knees at temples and pagodas.
- Take your shoes off before entering a temple or a home.
- In Thailand, never touch someone's head, and treat images of the royal family with respect.
- Choose wildlife experiences that do not involve riding or performing animals.
