# Central Vietnam: Where to Go and When to Visit

Central Vietnam is the narrow middle of the country, and for most travellers it means three places on the coast within about 150 km of each other: Hue, Da Nang and Hoi An, with the My Son ruins inland and the Phong Nha caves further north. Fly into Da Nang, give it five days or more, and go between February and May, avoiding October and November.

This is the easiest part of Vietnam to travel, because the distances are short and the road between Hue and Da Nang over the Hai Van Pass is a drive worth doing in itself. It also packs in more variety than its size suggests: an imperial capital, an old trading port, a modern beach city, Cham temple ruins and some of the largest caves on earth.

## Where is central Vietnam, and what cities are in it?

Central Vietnam runs roughly from Quang Binh province in the north, home of Phong Nha, down the coast through Hue, Da Nang and Quang Nam. For travel purposes, these are the places that matter:

| Place | What it is | From Da Nang |
|---|---|---|
| Da Nang | Beach city with the region's main airport | Base |
| Hoi An | UNESCO-listed old trading town of lanterns and shophouses | About 30 km south |
| Hue | Imperial capital from 1802 to 1945, the walled Citadel and royal tombs | About 100 km north, over or under the Hai Van Pass |
| My Son | Cham temple sanctuary in the hills, UNESCO-listed | Inland, west of Hoi An |
| Phong Nha | National park of giant caves | A long drive north of Hue |

**Is Hoi An in central Vietnam?** Yes, in Quang Nam province, just south of Da Nang. **Is Da Nang in central Vietnam?** Yes, it is the region's largest city and its transport hub. **Is Nha Trang central Vietnam?** It sits on the south-central coast, a long way further south, and most travellers group it with the south. The Central Highlands, around Da Lat, are an inland plateau with a cooler, different climate.

## What to do in central Vietnam

**Hue.** The walled Citadel on the Perfume River, Thien Mu Pagoda and the royal tombs scattered in the hills. The Citadel is large, flat and exposed, so a proper look takes two to three hours of walking. Go early.

**Hoi An.** The old town is best in the evening, when the lanterns are lit and the lantern boats go out on the Hoai River. By day, the countryside around it is the draw: basket boats in the Cam Thanh coconut groves, cooking classes at Tra Que vegetable village, and cycling through the rice fields.

**My Son.** The ruined temples of the Champa kingdom, in a forested valley inland from Hoi An. Early morning or late afternoon avoids the heat and the crowds.

**The Hai Van Pass.** The mountain road between Da Nang and Hue climbs to about 500 metres with the sea on one side the whole way. Most traffic now takes the tunnel underneath, which is why the pass itself is quiet. The name means Ocean Cloud Pass, and the summit is often in fog when the coast below is clear.

**Phong Nha.** Caves on a scale you will not see elsewhere, from Thien Duong, walked on a long boardwalk, to Son Doong, reached only on a multi-day expedition. Phong Nha deserves two nights or more of its own; the long drive there and back is the real cost.

## When to visit central Vietnam

The centre runs on a different calendar from the north and the south, and that is the thing Vietnam itineraries most often get wrong.

February to May is the best window: dry, warm and before the summer heat. June to August is hot but still mostly dry, which makes it the beach season in Da Nang and Hoi An. September brings rising rain, and October and November are the worst months. This coast takes the typhoons, and Hoi An's old town floods most years.

December and January are cooler and often grey and wet, especially in Hue, which is one of the rainiest cities in the country. Travel is possible, but plan indoor days.

## Central Vietnam floods: what to know

The floods come with the autumn rains and typhoons, most often in October and November. Hoi An's riverside streets go under water for a day or several, Hue can flood around the Citadel, and roads and the Hai Van Pass can close. Day tours are cancelled when the weather is bad.

If those months are your only option, keep your plans flexible, stay somewhere above the riverside in Hoi An, and buy travel insurance that covers weather disruption. The upside is fewer visitors and lower hotel prices.

## How many days do you need in central Vietnam?

Four days covers Hoi An and a day trip from Da Nang, but not Hue. Five days is the length where Hue and My Son both fit. Six days or more lets you add Phong Nha and the former DMZ, which means a long drive north. Eight days lets you take the pace out of it, with free afternoons rather than more sights.

Check the itinerary before you compare packages: on several five-day routes, the first and last days are travel days, so five days can mean three days of touring.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where should I base myself in central Vietnam?

Hoi An is the easiest base for most people: the old town, the countryside and My Son are all close, and Da Nang airport is about 45 minutes away. Stay a night or two in Hue if you want more than a day trip there. Da Nang suits travellers who want a city beach.

### Is it better to fly into Da Nang or Hue?

Da Nang, for most trips. It has the region's main airport, with far more domestic and international flights, and it sits between Hue and Hoi An. Hue's airport is small and Hoi An has none. Flying into Hue makes sense only if you are starting at the northern end of the region.

### Can I visit Hue as a day trip from Hoi An?

Yes, but it is a long day of about four hours in a vehicle, and the Citadel alone needs two to three hours on foot. Going via the Hai Van Pass makes the drive part of the day. If you have the time, one night in Hue is more comfortable and lets you see the tombs too.

### Is central Vietnam worth it if I only have two weeks in the country?

Yes. It sits between the two main entry points, so a north to south route passes through it anyway. Give it at least four or five of your fourteen days. For the rest of the region, the [Southeast Asia travel guide](README.md) links every country we cover.

Check your travel month against the February to May window first, then decide whether Phong Nha fits.
